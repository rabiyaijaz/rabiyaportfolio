How this becomes your game

The model is her appearance. The rig is the set of joints that move her. Animation clips describe each gesture. The controller decides when to play them.

This prototype uses articulated parts. For a closer likeness and smoother elbows, the next step is a sculpted mesh with a weighted skeleton. Your portfolio can keep the same action names.
